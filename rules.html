ROGUE CARDS

CANONICAL RULES
1–2 HEROES · d20 + d6
NINE ROOMS. ONE VAULT. THE CARDS TELL THE REST.

1 · The quest

Explore 3 UPPER, 3 MIDDLE and 3 DEEP Rooms, then the Vault. Trapdoors skip Rooms. Claim the Amulet to win; if no Hero can act, lose.

2 · Set up

  1. Choose 1–2 Heroes, starting Objects and HP d20s.
  2. Shuffle the Object deck (Items and Potions). Each Hero draws one. “Draw Item” means this deck.
  3. Shuffle separate Room and Monster piles by depth. Set aside Vault and Warden. Track HP with d20s. Never roll HP dice.

3 · In each Room

  1. REVEAL: Draw a Room; resolve its entry text.
  2. CHOOSE: Resolve one printed option; pay its cost.
  3. ENCOUNTER: PARLEY or fight. Only wins earn combat rewards; you may EAT its printed remains once.
  4. DESCEND: End Room effects, discard unless it says KEEP it, reset Room abilities. Enter Vault after DEEP.

4 · Combat turns

Heroes act in either order. Each turn: one action (attack, card action or pass). Triggers are free.

After each Hero turn, surviving Monsters roll d6 behaviour until one side falls or flees.

PARLEY: Follow its card. No reward for fleeing Monsters unless stated.

ELBERETH: Action: d20+WIT vs 15. Success: cancel next Monster response, or discard Item to escape. Failure: immediate ADV behaviour (no second response). Retry with DIS; second failure summons another Monster. Sentinel and Warden ignore it.

5 · Opposed d20 combat

d20 + ATKAttacker
d20 + DEFDefender

Both roll. Higher total hits; ties defend. Hits deal d6 damage. Attacking natural 1 always misses; natural 20 always hits and adds +d6.

WIT: Roll d20+WIT against printed DC, or opposed d20. Natural 1 fails; 20 succeeds.

ADV / DIS: Roll 2d20; keep higher / lower. They cancel.

6 · Objects and d6

d6: Resolve printed range. Monster behaviour uses 1–2 / 3–4 / 5 / 6. REROLL: When permitted, pay 1, 2, then 3 HP; new result stands. Printed complications apply.

Equip 1 Weapon, 1 Armour, 1 Ring and 1 Companion per Hero. Trade/equip freely between fights; healing still needs a printed effect.

Discard used Scrolls. Turn used Wands sideways until recharged. OVERCHARGE: Discard upright Wand, lose d6 HP; take printed result. Tags matter when named. WITH means carried; EQUIPPED means slotted. Only results marked TRAP count.

7 · What stays

POISONED: DIS until cured or Room end. Abilities reset each Room; HP never exceeds starting HP. KEEP: Retain the Room and resolve its later choice when printed.

Potions: Roll on first use; keep a copy face up. Identical Potions share the result.

TRAPDOOR: Skip remaining Rooms; resolve destination damage/Monsters. Cursed return: resolve one older Room d6, then resume DEEP.

At 0 HP, remove the die. An ally may heal them before leaving; otherwise lose if none can act.

8 · The final Vault

Roll OMEN d6. FIGHT or one-time SEAL. Kept Rooms may unlock SMUGGLE, BARGAIN or UNTHRONE after Phase I. Escape without Amulet is defeat.

Warden starts in Phase I; at 10 HP or fewer switch to Phase II, keeping the same HP die. Win at 0 HP, or via successful SEAL; failure means fight.

CARDS OVERRIDE THESE RULES WHEN THEY SAY SO. SPECIAL EFFECTS AND RESULTS ARE PRINTED ON THE CARDS. PRINT AT 100%.
