ROGUE CARDS

CANONICAL RULES
1–2 HEROES · d20 + d6
NINE ROOMS. ONE VAULT. THE CARDS TELL THE REST.

1 · The quest

Explore 3 UPPER, 3 MIDDLE and 3 DEEP Rooms, then the Amulet Vault. Defeat or seal the Warden to win. If no Hero can act, you lose.

2 · Set up

  1. Each player chooses a Hero and its starting Object. Set a d20 to its HP.
  2. Shuffle remaining Items and Potions into the Object deck. Each Hero draws 1 Object. “Draw Item” means this deck.
  3. Shuffle Room and Monster piles separately by region (UPPER / MIDDLE / DEEP). Set aside the Vault and Warden phases. Track Monster HP with separate d20s. Never roll HP dice.

3 · In each Room

  1. REVEAL: Draw the next Room. Resolve its entry text, including Monster draws.
  2. CHOOSE: As a party, resolve one printed Room option. Pay its cost, if any.
  3. ENCOUNTER: PARLEY with a remaining Monster or fight it. Take victory rewards only after winning; you may also EAT its printed remains once.
  4. DESCEND: End Room effects, discard the Room unless a card says KEEP it, and reset once-per-Room abilities. After Room 9, enter the Vault.

4 · Combat turns

Heroes act in either order. On each turn, take one action: attack, use a printed action on a card, or pass. Triggered abilities cost no action.

After each Hero turn, if the Monster survives, roll its d6 behaviour. Continue until the Monster leaves, falls, or the party falls.

PARLEY: Follow the Monster’s printed alternative to fighting. If it leaves, take no combat reward unless the card says otherwise.

5 · Opposed d20 combat

d20 + ATKAttacker
d20 + DEFDefender

Both sides roll. The attack hits only if the attacker’s total is higher; ties defend. A hit deals d6 damage. An attacking natural 1 always misses; a natural 20 always hits and adds +d6 damage. Monsters attack the same way.

WIT: Roll d20 + WIT against a printed DC (meet or beat it), or an opposed d20 as stated. Natural 1 fails; natural 20 succeeds.

ADV / DIS: Roll 2d20; keep the higher / lower result. They cancel each other.

6 · Objects and d6

d6: Find your roll in the printed result range and resolve it once. Monster behaviour uses 1–2 / 3–4 / 5 / 6.

Each Hero may equip 1 Weapon, 1 Armour, 1 Ring and 1 Companion. Carry other Objects freely. Outside combat, trade and equip freely; healing still needs a printed effect.

Scrolls are discarded after use. Wands turn sideways until recharged. OVERCHARGE: Discard an upright Wand and lose d6 HP for its stated result. Tags such as FIRE and CURSED matter when a card says so. WITH an Item means someone carries it; EQUIPPED means in a slot. Only Room results marked TRAP count as traps.

7 · What stays

POISONED: DIS on Hero d20 until cured or Room end. Once-per-Room abilities reset between Rooms; passive bonuses remain. HP cannot exceed starting HP.

Potions: Roll on first use. Keep a used Potion face up as a reminder; all Potions with the same name use that result this expedition.

At 0 HP, remove the die. That Hero cannot act, but an ally may heal them before leaving. If nobody can act, you lose.

8 · The final Vault

Roll the Vault’s OMEN d6. Choose FIGHT or a one-time SEAL attempt. Follow the card.

The Warden starts in Phase I. At 10 HP or fewer, switch to Phase II, keeping the same HP die. Reduce it to 0 HP to win. Successful SEAL wins; failure starts the printed fight.

CARDS OVERRIDE THESE RULES WHEN THEY SAY SO. SPECIAL EFFECTS AND RESULTS ARE PRINTED ON THE CARDS. PRINT AT 100%.
