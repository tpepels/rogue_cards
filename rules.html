ROGUE CARDS

CANONICAL RULES
1–2 HEROES · d20 + d6
ONE ROOM AT A TIME. ROLL FOR THE UNKNOWN. THE CARDS TELL THE REST.

1 · The quest

Play solo or together. Reveal 3 UPPER, 3 MIDDLE and 3 DEEP Rooms, then enter the fixed tenth Room, the Amulet Vault. Defeat the Warden or seal it to claim the Amulet and win. If no Hero can act, the expedition ends. No map or return journey.

2 · Set up

  1. Choose a Hero each. Set HP as printed. Find and take the Hero's printed starting Object, then draw 1 random Object from the remaining deck. Equip anything you can.
  2. Shuffle UPPER, MIDDLE and DEEP Room piles separately; shuffle their matching Monster piles. Set the Vault and both Warden phases aside.
  3. Shuffle remaining Items, Scrolls, Rings, Wands and Potions together as the Object deck. Take a d20, d6 and HP counters.

3 · In each Room

  1. REVEAL: Read and roll any entry effect. Follow printed instructions, including drawing Monsters.
  2. CHOOSE: Take exactly one printed Room option for the whole party. If a Monster is present, you may instead use its printed PARLEY before combat.
  3. FIGHT: Surviving Monsters must be fought unless the chosen option says otherwise. After combat take printed rewards and resolve safe effects.
  4. DESCEND: Discard the Room. End Room effects; straighten once-per-Room abilities. Reveal the next regional Room. After Room 9, reveal the Vault.

4 · Combat turns

Heroes act in either order each round. Each takes one action: attack, play a Scroll, use a Wand or Object, or a printed action; passing is allowed. A Hero ability used in response to its printed trigger is not an extra action. After every Hero turn, a living Monster rolls its d6 behaviour. Dead Monsters never respond.

Outside combat, trade Items, equip gear, and heal freely. Each Hero can equip 1 Weapon, 1 Armour, 1 Ring, 1 Companion; carry any number of other cards.

5 · Opposed d20 combat

d20 + ATKAttacker rolls
d20 + DEFDefender rolls

The higher total attacks successfully; a tie defends. On a hit, roll d6 damage. An attacking natural 1 misses; a natural 20 hits and deals +d6 damage. Monsters use the same rules. Printed card changes override normal results.

WIT: Roll d20 + WIT against printed DC (meet or beat it), or against an opposed d20 when printed. Natural 1 fails and natural 20 succeeds. ADV / DIS: roll 2d20, keep highest / lowest; they cancel.

6 · Roll ranges, magic, Monsters

ROLL d6: Find the result's printed band (1–2, 3–4, 5–6 or a printed four-band table). Resolve that effect once. Monster behaviours use 1–2 / 3–4 / 5 / 6.

Scrolls are discarded on use. Rings use one equipped slot. Wands turn sideways after use and stay exhausted until a card recharges them. OVERCHARGE means discard the upright Wand and lose d6 HP to take its stated result. Named properties such as FIRE and CURSED matter only when a card says so.

Monster PARLEY is an alternative to combat; pay the printed cost or roll the printed opposed d20. No combat reward or edible corpse unless it says so. Defeated Monsters may be eaten only when they print an EAT result.

7 · Things that stay

Use only HP counters and face-up, sideways or kept cards as reminders. POISONED: DIS on Hero d20 until cured or Room end. Once-per-Room abilities turn sideways; their passive bonuses still apply. Heal up to printed starting HP. Potions: roll on first use; keep one consumed Potion face up so identical names share its result this expedition.

At 0 HP, a Hero cannot act. An ally may heal them before leaving; if no Hero can act, you lose.

8 · The final Vault

Roll the Vault's d6 OMEN. Choose FIGHT or attempt its one-time SEAL bargain. In combat the Warden begins in Phase I; at 12 HP or fewer, replace its card with Phase II, keeping the same HP. Defeat Phase II at 0 HP to win. If SEAL succeeds, win without fighting.

ONLY UNIVERSAL RULES ARE HERE. ALL SPECIAL CHOICES, MONSTER BEHAVIOURS AND ROLL RANGES ARE ON THE CARDS. PRINT AT 100%.
