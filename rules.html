ROGUE CARDS

TABLETOP EDITION 1
CORE RULES · A4 · 1–2 PLAYERS
ONE ROOM AT A TIME. ONE ACTION PER HERO. NO COMPUTER.

1 · YOUR QUEST

Solo or cooperative: descend through nine Rooms, defeat the Warden in the tenth Room, and take the Amulet. You win immediately. If all Heroes die, the expedition ends. No return journey, map, or dungeon movement rules.

2 · SET UP

  1. Choose one Hero each (up to two); take its card. Record the starting HP. Each Hero draws two Items from the shuffled Item/Potion deck. Equip starting gear freely.
  2. Shuffle UPPER, MIDDLE and DEEP Room cards as three separate piles. You will reveal three of each, in that order, then the fixed Amulet Vault.
  3. Shuffle matching UPPER, MIDDLE and DEEP Monster cards into three separate piles. Put the Warden aside for the last Room. Keep one d20 and one d6 per player or share them, pencil and HP markers nearby.
  4. Start by revealing the first UPPER Room. Do what its card says.

3 · EVERY ROOM

REVEAL: Read and resolve the Room card. If instructed, draw its region's Monster. Only that monster is active. Resolve immediate hazards and rewards printed on the Room.

ENCOUNTER: If a Monster is present, take turns in player order: each Hero may perform one action. After every Hero action, the living Monster rolls a d6 and resolves its behaviour against the active Hero. Repeat rounds until it dies or both Heroes are down.

AFTERWARD: Take rewards printed on the Room (draw from the finite Item/Potion pile). Each Hero may make one safe action (use, equip, eat, trade, pray if allowed). Optional actions printed on the Room count as that action. Then discard the Room and reveal the next one. There is no backtracking.

4 · YOUR ACTIONS

ATTACK a Monster. CAST / DRINK / USE / EQUIP a card, or TRADE one Item with another Hero. You may also use an action printed on your Hero or Room. A defeated Monster's corpse may be EATEN once as a safe action; resolve its CORPSE text. Keep the Monster card tucked under your Hero only if it grants a lasting trait.

Items received from Rooms are free to collect; an Item used during a fight costs an action. Carry up to five Items per Hero; discard extras. Each Hero equips one Weapon, one Armour and one Companion. Replacing equipment is an action; CURSED gear cannot be removed except as printed on Altar cards.

5 · OPPOSED d20 COMBAT

The attacker rolls d20 + ATK; the defender rolls d20 + DEF. The attack hits only if the attacker's total is higher. On a hit, roll d6 damage and subtract that much HP. Monster attacks use the same method.

Natural 1: automatic miss.
Natural 20: automatic hit; roll an extra d6 damage.
Tie: defender wins.
Attack +2: add 2 to the attack total.

A card may replace ATK with WIT or change damage. Both Heroes and Monsters can receive modifiers; all effects are printed on their cards. Heal no higher than starting HP.

6 · d6 MONSTER BEHAVIOUR

After each Hero action, roll d6 and follow the matching line on the Monster card (1–2, 3–4, 5, or 6). ATTACK means roll opposed d20, then d6 damage if it hits. Unless a card says otherwise, the active Hero defends. If a Monster is defeated by the Hero's action, it cannot respond.

7 · SAVES, MAGIC & CONDITIONS

A Room or card may demand a WIT save: roll d20 + the Hero's WIT against its printed DC. Meet or exceed the DC to succeed. On a natural 1 fail; natural 20 succeeds.

ADV
Roll 2d20; use higher.
DIS
Roll 2d20; use lower. ADV and DIS cancel.
POISONED
−2 on the Hero's d20 rolls until cured.
Next roll
Use the modifier once, then clear it.
Room
Effects marked ONCE PER ROOM reset with each Room.

Potions with an unknown effect resolve their first-use d6 roll on that Potion card. Write the result and keep the first consumed Potion face up as an identification reminder: all other Potions with the same name use the same result for this expedition. No hidden tables.

8 · CO-OP & THE END

Two Heroes share the objective but track separate HP, gear and conditions. They may freely discuss plans. At 0 HP a Hero is down and cannot act; a teammate may restore them with an Item before leaving the Room. In solo play, reaching 0 HP ends the game. If both are down, the party loses.

The tenth Room is the Amulet Vault. Defeat its Warden and take the Amulet: victory. There is no final return journey.
