NOTHING IS
WHAT IT SEEMS.

Phase 3 introduces four separate families of unidentified objects: potions, scrolls, wands, and rings. Only actions printed on an item are legal. There is no freeform spell combination, dipping or invented interaction.

SETUP / FOUR HIDDEN MAPPINGS

  1. Set aside the Identity cards, grouped by family. These are reference cards, not loot. There are five potion identities (small cut-out slips on page 3 of the main Field Guide) and four each of Scroll, Wand and Ring identities.
  2. For each family separately, shuffle its identity cards face down. Place one under each named appearance in the order shown below. Do not peek. Every appearance receives exactly one identity.
  3. Shuffle ordinary appearance cards into the Object supply, while keeping Identity cards outside that supply. Rings have three printed copies per appearance, explicitly marked UNCURSED / BLESSED / CURSED; these markers are visible and do not change the hidden identity.
  4. When you use an unknown item, reveal its matching hidden identity card, perform the printed action, and write its identity below. Leave it face up. Other objects with the same appearance share the same identity for the entire run.
  5. Wands start with three charges per individual wand card. Mark each charge when ZAP is used; discard the wand after its third use. A Ring occupies the one Ring slot; exchanging rings takes one action. A scroll is discarded after READ.

FIFTEEN
PROPERTIES.

Properties are a shared vocabulary, not fifteen independent subsystems. A card only triggers the interactions listed in the reference below or its own printed action. No effect is invented merely because two tags sound compatible.

FIRE Flame scroll: 4 damage; WATER reduces to 1.
WATER +2 electricity and thrown frost damage; reduces FIRE.
COLD Frost wand: 2 damage and freeze one monster turn.
ELECTRICITY Sparks wand: 3 damage, +2 in WATER.
ACID Acid wand: 2 damage, +2 versus METAL.
POISON Poison potions harm; Resistance ring prevents self-poison.
METAL Rust Monster and Warden; vulnerable to acid.
WOOD Wooden rooms and furnishings; currently descriptive.
STONE Trap and locked passages; Digging bypasses their effects.
DARK -1 to Hero attack in dark rooms, unless Light ring works.
LIGHT A functioning Light ring suppresses DARK penalty.
HOLY Warding scroll reduces two successful hits by 2 each; Warden resists Banishment.
DIGGING Wand charges bypass the next TRAP or LOCKED effect.
TELEPORT Banishment permanently removes a non-Warden monster.
ORGANIC Organic creature classification; currently descriptive.

IMPORTANT RESTRICTIONS

DRINK / THROW only on Potions. READ only on Scrolls. ZAP only on Wands. WEAR only on Rings. One action per use. No unprinted combinations.

Scroll Mapping peeks at the top Room card for an unexplored adjacent location; it does not explore or trigger that location. Wand Digging prepares one bypass for the next locked or trapped room you enter. No spontaneous passage is created. Attacking a Monster or ending a turn still uses the original Phase 2 rules.
