The existing two-action turn, d6 hit roll, shared ten-card monster Behaviour deck, room exits, and all Phase 3–5 systems remain. These tactical actions cost one action each. No extra dice or miniatures are needed.

1 / REVEALED INTENT

When facing a live monster, look at the top of the Behaviour draw pile without taking it. That card is the monster's next intention. After your actions, resolve the normal monster turn and take that card. If the draw pile is empty, intention is UNKNOWN: reshuffle the discard when the monster would act. Invisibility, freezing and blinding still suppress Behaviour as previously.

3 / ENCHANTING AND CURSED EQUIPMENT

When a newly discovered Sword or Mail enters a room's Object supply, roll one d6 to record its modifier: 1 = CURSED -1, 2–5 = +1, 6 = +2. Positive or negative values replace the normal +1 stat bonus. Keep the mark on that individual card.

At an Altar, once per room, spend one action to ENCHANT one equipped Sword or Mail: d6=1 makes it CURSED -1; 2–4 increases its bonus by 1; 5–6 increases it by 2. Maximum bonus +3. A cursed item cannot be unequipped. Instead use that Altar's once-per-room action to CLEANSE: suffer 2 HP, remove CURSED, restore the item's bonus to +1.

Enchanting is not a free extra action. Risking the altar prevents using it again for another enchantment or cleansing in that room.

4 / ENVIRONMENTAL PUZZLES

A Crypt, Fungal Nest, or Flooded Chamber has a hidden cache. After any monster is defeated, spend one action to solve the cache once: discard a Key, spend one prepared DIGGING bypass, or lose 2 Hunger. Then take the top remaining Object directly from the printed Object pile. Mark the room's cache as opened. This does not replace the room's ordinary loot.

Other hazards remain as before: traps and locks trigger on first entry; the wand of Digging bypasses either; WATER boosts electricity and frost; DARK affects attacks and Light; corpses and pet scavenging still follow the Living Dungeon guide. There are no unprinted free-form combinations.

5 / BARGAINS AND OPTIONAL RISKS

In a Ruined Shop or Abandoned Altar, after clearing the monster, you may take one BARGAIN action per room: lose 2 HP to take one remaining Object from the supply. This can kill you. The once-per-room bargain is separate from an Altar's enchantment action and the existing Prayer cooldown. If no Object cards remain, the bargain yields nothing.
