ROGUE CARDS V2

ONE-PAGE PLAYTEST RULES
1–2 HEROES · d20 + d6
ONE ROOM AT A TIME. ROLL FOR THE UNKNOWN. THE CARDS TELL THE REST.

1 · The quest

Play solo or together. Descend through 3 UPPER, 3 MIDDLE and 3 DEEP Rooms, then enter the fixed tenth Room: the Amulet Vault. Defeat its Warden to win. If all Heroes are down, you lose. No map, backtracking or return journey.

2 · Set up

  1. Choose one Hero each, set their starting HP, draw 2 Items per Hero. Equip any starting gear.
  2. Shuffle the three Room piles separately, and shuffle the three corresponding Monster piles. Set the Amulet Vault and Warden aside for Room 10.
  3. Shuffle Items and Potions into one Object deck. Bring a d20, d6 and HP counters. Reveal your first UPPER Room.

3 · A Room

  1. REVEAL. Read the Room card. Make any entry rolls and draw a Monster if instructed.
  2. FIGHT. If a Monster is present, resolve combat before exploring, unless a card says otherwise.
  3. EXPLORE. Collect rewards and interact with the Room's objects or choices. Each printed interaction may be resolved once per Room unless its card says otherwise. No general limit on safe actions.
  4. DESCEND. Discard the Room and reveal the next one. Clear temporary Room effects.

4 · Hero turns

During combat, each Hero takes one action in player order: ATTACK, use a Spell or Item, equip, or perform a printed ability. A Hero may pass. After each Hero turn, a surviving Monster rolls its own d6 behaviour and responds to that Hero. If the Hero's action killed it, it does not respond.

Outside combat, using or exchanging cards and changing equipment is free. No inventory limit. Each Hero may equip one Weapon, one Armour and one Companion; other restrictions appear on cards.

5 · Opposed d20 combat

d20 + ATKAttacker rolls
d20 + DEFDefender rolls

The attacker hits only with the higher total; ties defend. On a hit, roll d6 damage and subtract HP. A natural 1 on the attack misses; natural 20 hits automatically and deals an extra d6. Monsters attack the same way. Any modifiers or alternatives are printed on cards.

6 · Roll for things

WIT test: roll d20 + WIT against a printed DC; meet or beat it. Natural 1 fails, natural 20 succeeds. ADV: roll 2d20, keep higher. DIS: keep lower. They cancel.

Rooms, Monsters, Items, Potions and objects can tell you to ROLL d6. Read the matching range on that card. Each roll resolves once unless the card says otherwise; there are no separate lookup tables.

Example — DRINK FROM THE FOUNTAIN · ROLL d6 1–2: Suffer 2 damage
3–4: Heal d6 HP
5–6: Draw 1 Item

7 · Things that stay

Keep HP counters and the cards themselves as your only trackers. POISONED: DIS on d20 rolls until cured or this Room ends. Other temporary effects end at the time printed on their card. Heal only up to starting HP.

Turn a card sideways after using an ONCE PER ROOM ability; straighten it at the next Room. EAT a defeated Monster only if it prints an EAT effect. For mystery Potions, roll on first use and keep that used Potion visible: later Potions of the same name use its discovered result.

8 · Down, but not out

At 0 HP, a Hero cannot act until healed. Another Hero can use a healing effect to revive them. If no Hero can act, the expedition ends. In Room 10, defeat the Warden and claim the Amulet: victory.
