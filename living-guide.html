Use the original dungeon, two-action turn, d6 combat and monster behaviour rules unchanged. Phase 5 adds a separate Living Dungeon reference pile; these cards are not shuffled into the loot deck. Track each pet, corpse, intrinsic, and prayer result with a pencil.

01 / AUTONOMOUS COMPANION

EQUIP the Faithful Dog as one action. At the end of every turn, before monster behaviour, check the Dog. Every third turn reduce Dog hunger by 1. If Dog hunger reaches 0 it wanders off. Feed a ration as one action to restore Dog hunger to 9 and raise loyalty by 1 (maximum 4).

Roll d6Pet action, when present in the current room
1–2ROAM: loyalty -1 (minimum 0). Dog searches; does not move monsters.
3–4SNIFF: loyalty +1 (maximum 4). Can signal an old trap.
5–6ATTACK: if a monster is present, deal 1 damage. Otherwise rest.

When you change rooms the Dog catches up on its next turn-end instead of rolling. It is autonomous: do not add the previous Phase 2 passive +1 bonus to your melee attack.

02 / CORPSES: MEAT WITH CONSEQUENCES

When an ordinary monster dies, place one species Corpse marker in that room (unless it already has one). COLLECT it for one action; EAT it for another. Corpse freshness is measured in turns since death. After 6 turns a corpse is stale: restore two fewer hunger (minimum 1), and illness is more likely.

04 / MONSTER ECOLOGY

Monsters stay in their original rooms (Phase 4). Habitat affinity grants a newly encountered monster +1 maximum HP and +1 current HP if its preferred terrain matches the room. These traits use existing printed terrain labels.

05 / PRAY: A RISKY ACTION ANYWHERE

Prayer takes one normal action. You may pray in any room, including while in danger. Roll d6 + Favour (+1 if at an Altar). Start at Favour 0, range -2 to +2.

On a total of 4 or more, restore 3 HP (4 at an Altar), then Favour +1. Otherwise lose 2 Hunger and Favour -1. After praying, do not pray again until six completed turns have passed. The altar no longer gives automatic healing.

06 / BONES: WHAT THE PREVIOUS HERO LEFT

On death, write a Bones Record: dead Hero class and up to three uneaten objects carried. Save it between games. On the next expedition, the first newly revealed room at depth 3–9 is eligible. Roll d6 once: on 5–6, leave the first recorded object in that room as Bones loot. It costs one action to reclaim. Otherwise the remains are never found this run. The record is spent after that attempt.
