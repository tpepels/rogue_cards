# Printed card copy audit

Scope: all **111 unique cards** on the canonical 90 × 130 mm cards.
Counts cover instructions, die outcomes, Room prompts and Monster alternatives;
they exclude titles, tags, flavour and other decorative labels.

## Editorial pass

- Mechanical-copy length: **5,148 → 4,996 words** (around 45 per card).
- **0 cards** exceed the 70-word review threshold after edits.
- Forty-six specific phrase rewrites standardise actions and remove redundant lead-ins.
- Rusty Key and Knotted Rope no longer repeat their general, already printed interactions.
- Potion identification is printed once, not twice. No d6 range, effect handler, card statistic or card art changed.
- The `REVIEWED_CARD_HASHES` snapshot is updated only after checking all typed effect handlers and sample simulations.

## Wording that needs a gameplay ruling

These are questions about **mechanics**, not merely prose. Their text remains unchanged rather than guessing at the intended rule.

- **Marsh Witch** (`hero:witch`): Timing of HEX BREW versus resolving the Potion or Scroll.
- **Forgotten Supplies** (`room:u-supply`): Where is the returned Item placed? Simulation discards rather than explicitly returning.
- **Lost Reliquary** (`room:d-reliquary`): Which printed effects qualify as a 'trap'?
- **Cave Bat Swarm** (`monster:bat`): How does DIS apply to a Hero action without a d20 roll?
- **Floating Eye** (`monster:eye`): How does DIS apply to a Hero action without a d20 roll?
- **Bridge Troll** (`monster:troll`): What precisely counts as scorched by FIRE, and for how long?
- **Ashwood Staff** (`item:staff`): Which attacks count as a spell attack made with WIT?
- **Pitch Torch** (`item:torch`): Which Rooms qualify as dark?
- **Wand of Almost Doors** (`item:wand-door`): Which Rooms count as locked? The card lacks a printed named list.
