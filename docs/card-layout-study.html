Before final art or bulk printing

Approve the shared frame, the 11 specialised interiors, the Room's ENTER / HERE / CLEAR verbs, the Monster's four-line behaviour panel, and how the three mystery identities are marked using only orientation. Then produce one *fully illustrated* card per type with unique woodcut artwork, verify print legibility in hand, and only afterward regenerate the rest of the deck. These eleven cards are exploratory wireframes, not new current cards.
