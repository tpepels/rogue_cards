illustration in this style:

Forgotten Depths

Lo-fi, printed, dark late-1980s to mid-1990s fantasy computer-game / RPG zine illustration. Strong old-school Rogue-inspired feeling. Crude dungeon-fantasy imagery, slightly archaic and less modern-looking.

The images should feel like cheap printed game illustrations reproduced on old damaged paper: worn, dirty, imperfect, and low-quality. Printed, damaged paper texture is important: ink spread, rough halftone or bitmap texture, print wear, photocopy degradation, imperfect registration, grime, and scan-like dirt. The roughness should come mainly from the reproduction process, not from overly busy drawing detail.

Color: black + off-white paper + one accent color (e.g. magenta, cyan, acid green, orange). Accent color should be used boldly but simply.

Composition: the illustration should fill the entire card background vertically. Design it to work as a background image for a card, with a strong central silhouette or focal subject and clear readable forms. Keep it iconic and uncluttered.

Important constraints:
- no text in/on the images
- no modern polished digital look
- no clean contemporary fantasy concept-art rendering
- no card frame or typography
- no extra decorative UI
- no excessive detail
- no smooth gradients
- no more than 3 main value levels
- full-card vertical illustration
- no borders, the image should cover the entire canvas
