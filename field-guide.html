THE RULES OF
DYING UNDERGROUND

SOLO PROTOTYPE / 1 PLAYER / 1 D6 / TARGET: 90–120 MINUTES (NOT YET TIMED IN PHYSICAL PLAY)

Descend ten depths, defeat the Amulet Warden, claim the Amulet of Yendor, and return to the entrance alive. The dungeon is persistent: rooms, dropped loot and wounded monsters remain when you leave. If HP reaches zero, you lose.

01 / EQUIPMENT & SETUP

Print the PHYSICAL KIT from the Cards page, along with this guide and the two tracking sheets. Bring one six-sided die, a pencil and markers for HP/hunger.

  1. Pick one Hero. Set HP, ATK and DEF to printed values; hunger to 18 (maximum 24).
  2. Put Dungeon Entrance at depth 0 and Vault of Yendor aside for depth 10. Keep the Warden and Amulet cards beside the Vault.
  3. Make separate Room piles marked UPPER (depths 1–3), MIDDLE (4–6), DEEP (7–9), plus Monsters (except Warden), Objects (rations, keys, equipment and potions), Events, Behaviours, and a set-aside Amulet Warden.
  1. Make the ten-card Behaviour pile: Strike ×3, Lunge ×2, Guard ×2, Frenzy ×1, Drain ×1, Recover ×1.
  2. Give the Hero starting items from the Object pile: Delver 2 rations; Knight 1 ration; Wizard 3 random potions; Rogue 1 ration, 1 key and 1 smoky potion. Shuffle the remaining Object pile.
  3. Shuffle the five potion identity slips (page 3) face down. Assign one to each potion appearance on the tracking sheet without looking.
  4. Set turn 1, actions 2. Start at entrance.

02 / HOW THE DUNGEON CONNECTS

The dungeon is a single main route from depth 0 to 10. Depths 1–9 each have an optional side chamber that connects only to that depth's main room. Draw rooms only when exploring an unknown location. Returning to a previously visited room uses one Move action; it never redraws its contents.

Use the dungeon sheet to record which main and side locations exist. Keep only the active room, monster, and loot beside your Hero. Record unrecovered items and monster HP in the location boxes when leaving. You may retreat from combat only toward the entrance into a known connected room. A monster otherwise blocks further exploration.

03 / A TURN — EXACTLY THE SAME IN THE BROWSER

TAKE UP TO TWO ACTIONS → LOSE 1 HUNGER → RESOLVE ONE MONSTER BEHAVIOUR CARD IF A MONSTER IS IN YOUR ROOM → BEGIN A NEW TURN WITH TWO ACTIONS.

You can end your turn with zero or one action. An action can be: explore, move, melee attack, take loot, drink, throw, eat, equip, consume, pray, use a fountain, or claim the Amulet. Each costs one action. If hunger is 0 after the turn's hunger loss, lose 1 HP from starvation. Resolve starvation before any monster behaviour.

04 / ENTERING AN UNKNOWN LOCATION

For each new main or side location, draw from the matching regional Room pile and record the exits and room on the map. All main rooms connect backwards and forwards; a printed SIDE mark allows a side chamber. LINK on adjacent revealed side chambers forms a shortcut. Depth 10 is always the Vault of Yendor with the Amulet Warden, not a random Room. Its defeat is required before claiming the Amulet.

Discard a defeated Monster to the Monster discard pile; leave its card face up in the location until recorded. Reshuffle Monster, Event, and Behaviour discards when their draw piles empty. When no objects remain, a new room provides no loot.

05 / D6 COMBAT

ROLL d6 + ATTACK VS 4 + DEFENCE. NATURAL 1 = MISS. NATURAL 6 = HIT FOR 3 DAMAGE. OTHER HITS = 2 DAMAGE.

For a Hero attack, use the Hero's ATK and the Monster's DEF. A weapon adds +1 ATK, while Guard adds +1 to Monster DEF until its next behaviour. For a Monster attack, use Monster ATK and Hero DEF; equipped mail adds +1 Hero DEF. An attack with modifiers still uses the natural-1 and natural-6 rules.

Example: Delver ATK 3 attacks a Rust Monster DEF 1. A d6 result of 2 gives 5 vs target 5: the attack hits for 2 HP. A natural 1 misses; a natural 6 hits for 3 HP. Damage is deducted from the target's HP.

Monster traits: Goblin's first successful strike deals +1 damage. A Rust Monster's successful strike reduces base Hero DEF by 1 (minimum 0). A Restless Shade's hit also removes 1 hunger. Attacking a surviving Floating Eye in melee costs the Hero 1 HP. A companion Dog adds +1 damage to its first successful melee hit against each monster.

07 / SUPPLIES AND THE RETURN JOURNEY

Ration: Eat to restore 7 hunger, up to 24. Lucky Bone: Consume to restore 3 HP. Key: Used automatically on first entry to a locked or trapped room, if carried.

Equipment: Spend an action to equip a Notched Sword (+1 ATK) or Patchwork Mail (+1 DEF). Only one weapon and one armour may be equipped; return the old one to your inventory when replacing it. The Dog occupies a single companion slot and assists on your first successful melee hit against each monster.

Altar: Pray once per altar to restore 2 HP. Fountain: Use once per fountain. On d6 1–3 heal 3 HP; on 4–6 take a randomly selected remaining potion from the Object supply (none if exhausted).

After defeating the Warden, claim the Amulet for one action, then return along previously visited rooms. You cannot reveal new locations during the return. Enter depth 0 with the Amulet to win.

08 / IDENTIFICATION WITHOUT A COMPUTER

Cut out the five identity slips below. Shuffle them face down. Assign one beneath each appearance in order: Smoky, Milky, Bubbling, Black, Cloudy. When you drink or throw a potion, reveal its slip, resolve the appropriate effect, and record that appearance in your identification notebook. Subsequent potions of the same appearance share that identity. Never reshuffle the five assignments during a run.

09 / EVENTS & RECORD KEEPING

Strange Feeling: lose 1 hunger. Bones of Someone: gain a ration from the supply, if one remains. Whispers Below: lose 2 hunger. Cold Draft: restore 1 HP. Draw events only when a new non-Vault room is revealed on an event-roll 6.

Write down a room's name, monster HP, and untouched loot when leaving it. Monsters stay in the room where encountered and never pursue. Re-entering a room has no new room, encounter, loot, trap, or event roll. Each turn, mark action 1 or 2; resolve hunger and one behaviour, then clear the action marks.

Phase 3: Shuffle unidentified Scroll, Wand and Ring appearance cards into the Object deck as well. Each has its own matching hidden Identity pile and reference sheet. Read The Unknown Objects ↗ for the fifteen properties, Ring blessings/curses, and exact READ / ZAP / WEAR actions.

Scope: This is a Phase 2 physical-first prototype. Session length and card economy require table-timed playtests. All v2 card effects should be resolved using these rules, without consulting a computer.
