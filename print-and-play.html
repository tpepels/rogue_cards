ROGUE CARDS / PHASE 7 / PRINT-AND-PLAY

One dungeon. One life.

A complete physical setup and reference index for the ten-depth Rogue Cards expedition. The browser and the printed kit share the same dice, cards, room routes, hidden identities and survival rules.

Setup

  1. Print all cards at 100% / actual size, A4, no Fit-to-Page scaling. Physical cards are 90 × 140 mm. Print the map and references separately.
  2. Choose one of four heroes. Remove starting equipment from the finite Object supply as prescribed by the hero.
  3. Separate and shuffle the Upper Works (depth 1–3), Flooded Hollows (4–6), and Black Crypts (7–9) Room piles. Place fixed Entrance (0) and Vault (10) face-up.
  4. Shuffle Monster, Object, Event and Behaviour piles separately, keeping the Warden aside for the Vault.
  5. Shuffle each hidden potion, scroll, wand and ring identity assignment independently. All copies of a given appearance share the same hidden identity throughout this run.
  6. Record HP, Hunger, turn, actions, pet food, prayer favour and cooldown, intrinsics and equipment on the map or notes. Use only the trackers for systems actually encountered.

At the table

  1. Each turn take up to two actions, or end early. Exploring an unknown room reveals it upon entry; note its exits, encounters and objects. Revisited rooms preserve their state.
  2. When facing a monster, reveal the top Behaviour card as its intention; use attack, brace, feint, class technique, magic or retreat as permitted. Resolve dice as printed, then monster Behaviour at turn end.
  3. Lose Hunger at end of turn, resolve the Dog (if equipped), monster response, cooldowns and statuses. Death is permanent; the Amulet must be retrieved at depth 10 and carried back to the Entrance.

One-page rule hierarchy

Core guide governs turns, dice, objects, room geometry and victory. Unknown Objects governs magical identities and explicit property effects. Living Dungeon governs pets, corpses, intrinsic traits, prayer and bones. Tactical Survival governs monster intentions, hero techniques, gear enchantments, caches, bargains and deeper pressure. Card-specific instructions take priority only where they explicitly amend these shared rules.
