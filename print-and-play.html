PHYSICAL FIRST / PRINT GUIDE

Print, cut, play.

Nothing in the current edition needs a web browser, app, spreadsheet, virtual map, AI, or extra rulebook during play.

PRINT CARDS ↗PRINT ONE A4 OF RULES ↗

What you need

  • A4 printer and paper or card stock; scissors or cutter
  • A d20 and a d6 (shared dice are enough)
  • Pencil, small counters for Hero/Monster HP, and scrap paper if desired
  • The card kit and exactly one A4 sheet of common rules

Print the complete deck

  1. Open the card gallery. Choose COMPLETE KIT and keep PRINT QUANTITIES selected. Click PRINT THIS DECK.
  2. Set your printer to A4 portrait, 100% / actual size, background graphics on. Disable fit-to-page and browser-added headers and footers. Each card is 90 × 130 mm, four per A4.
  3. Print the core rules on one separate A4 page. No other rule sheets are required.
  4. Cut cards along the black borders. Optional: sleeve with opaque backs to conceal shuffled card identities.

Separate these piles

  • Room: UPPER, MIDDLE, DEEP. Shuffle each region separately. Deal three UPPER Rooms, then three MIDDLE, then three DEEP, revealing them one by one. Place the Amulet Vault last.
  • Monster: UPPER, MIDDLE, DEEP. Shuffle each pile separately. Keep the Amulet Warden for the final Vault.
  • Items + Potions: Shuffle together into one finite Object deck. Each Hero starts with two draws.
  • Heroes: Each player chooses one; solo play uses one Hero.

How to track cards without reference sheets

Record Hero HP with counters. Put equipped Weapon, Armour and Companion alongside the Hero. Put permanent corpse abilities under the Hero. Mark ONCE PER ROOM effects by turning a card sideways; straighten cards when moving automatically to the next Room. Keep the first used mystery Potion face-up to remember the d6 result for every Potion with the same name.

Design target: 90–120 minutes. Real tabletop playtime and final card readability remain to be tested. The earlier multi-document digital-prototype rules are archived in Git history, not needed to play this edition.
