Three things to keep straight

HP dice are counters

Keep one d20 on each Hero and active Monster showing remaining HP. Do not roll those dice. Use other d20s for attacks and WIT.

Read the card in front of you

Each Room gives two choices and printed results. Each Monster has its own d6 behaviour and PARLEY. Objects explain their own timing and costs.

One action in combat

On a Hero turn: attack, take a printed card action, or pass. If the Monster survives, roll its behaviour after that turn. Triggered abilities cost no action.
